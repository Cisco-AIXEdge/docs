AIXEdge

In the dynamic landscape of network infrastructure, where agility and responsiveness are paramount, Cisco introduces AIXEdge revolutionary addition to the IOS XE / NX-OS platforms. AIXEdge is engineered to redefine the way network administrators interact with and manage Cisco IOS XE / NX-OS based devices. By combining cutting-edge technologies, AI-XEdge promises to provide helpful assitance during the configuration of device and not only!

Demo

This demonstration showcases the powerful capabilities and diverse possibilities of using AIXEdge on IOS XE & NX-OS. It illustrates how AIXEdge enhances user interaction with IOS XE by providing intelligent guidance, multilingual support, and an interactive approach to querying and configuring devices.

  • 🔧 Configuration Assistance: Users can query the large language model (LLM) for step-by-step guidance on configuring various features and settings on an IOS XE device.
  • 🌍 Multilingual Support: AIXEdge enables users to receive configuration instructions and troubleshooting assistance in different languages, making it more accessible for global teams.
  • 💬 Interactive Conversational Interface: Instead of relying solely on traditional commands, users can engage in a dynamic conversation with their IOS XE device, asking questions and receiving contextual responses to streamline configuration and troubleshooting.

Commands

  • aixedge <query> - Queries adressed to AI Assistant
  • aixedge <show command> @ <query to AI assistant> - AI Assistant helps with command's output
  • aixedge-optics <query> - Queries AI Assistant regarding optics & compatibility
  • aixedge-feature <query> - Queries AI Assistant regarding compatibility between features and platform
  • aixedge-upgrade - Upgrades the AI Assistant;
  • aixedge-cfg <API_KEY> - Initialization of AI assistant
  • aixedge-init - Initialization of AI assistant
  • aixedge-version - Shows the current software version
  • aixedge-uninstall - Remove AIXEdge (config etc.)
