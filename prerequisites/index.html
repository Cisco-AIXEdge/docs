Cisco IOS XE Copilot on Catalyst 9000 Switching
  • Prerequisites

Prerequisites

Software

Supported

  • IOS XE 17.9+

Hardware

Supported

  • AppGigEthernet (KR)
    • Catalyst 9300
    • Catalyst 9400

Not supported (Manual config)

  • Mgmt VRF (OOB)
    • Catalyst 9500
    • Catalyst 9600

Network

  • Internet Connectivity (works also behind proxy)
  • Client VLAN for Copilot (e.g like endpoints) (no VLAN 1)
  • DHCP/Static IP for Guestshell (attached to Client VLAN)
